BCS508

Environmental Studies MCQ

Important MCQ (50 marks)

MODULE 1

Repeated 3 Times

  • Q1. In what form is solar energy radiated from the Sun?
    Answer: Electromagnetic waves

Repeated 2 Times

  • Q2. A food web consists of:
    Answer: Interlocking food chains
  • Q3. In an ecosystem, biological cycling of materials is maintained by:
    Answer: All of these
    Producers, Consumers, Decomposers
  • Q4. Which ecosystem is associated with both running water and stagnant water / frequent flooding and water saturation?
    Answer: Wetlands
  • Q5. How many Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are there?
    Answer: 17

Important One-Time Questions

  • Q6. Which of the following best defines an ecosystem?
    Answer: Interaction of organisms and physical environment
  • Q7. What is the primary characteristic of wetlands?
    Answer: Frequent flooding and water saturation
  • Q8. Which ecosystem is characterized by high biodiversity, tall trees and a warm climate year-round?
    Answer: Forest ecosystem
  • Q9. What is the primary goal of SDG 1?
    Answer: Eradicate poverty
  • Q10. SDG 13 focuses on:
    Answer: Climate action
  • Q11. SDG 14 focuses on the conservation and sustainable use of:
    Answer: Oceans, seas and marine resources

MODULE 2

Repeated 2 Times

  • Q1. What type of energy is wind energy?
    Answer: Renewable energy
  • Q2. In what form is solar energy radiated from the Sun?
    Answer: Electromagnetic waves

Important One-Time Questions

  • Q3. Which of the following energy sources has the greatest potential among renewable energy sources?
    Answer: Solar energy
  • Q4. What does OTEC stand for?
    Answer: Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion
  • Q5. What is the by-product of Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion (OTEC)?
    Answer: Desalinated water
  • Q6. Open-cycle OTEC uses surface water that is:
    Answer: Warm
  • Q7. Which type of turbine is commonly used in tidal energy?
    Answer: Gorlov turbine
  • Q8. What is a fuel cell?
    Answer: Chemical-to-electrical conversion
  • Q9. Which of the following is a non-conventional source of energy?
    Answer: Solar energy
  • Q10. Hydropower is considered a type of:
    Answer: Renewable energy
  • Q11. Hydrogen is considered a clean energy source because:
    Answer: It produces only water as a by-product
  • Q12. What is the allowable concentration of fluoride in drinking water according to the WHO question in the Model Paper?
    Answer: 1.5 mg/L
  • Q13. What disease is caused by drinking water containing high levels of nitrates?
    Answer: Methemoglobinemia

MODULE 3

Repeated 3 Times

  • Q1. What is the primary cause of acid rain?
    Answer: Sulphur dioxide (SO₂)

Repeated 2 Times

  • Q2. Minamata disease is caused by:
    Answer: Mercury

Important One-Time Questions

  • Q3. BOD stands for:
    Answer: Biochemical Oxygen Demand
  • Q4. Which unit is used to measure noise level?
    Answer: Decibel (dB)
  • Q5. Which is a major source of water pollution?
    Answer: Agricultural runoff
  • Q6. Which is a major source of air pollution in urban areas?
    Answer: Automobile emissions
  • Q7. What is the major effect of excessive nitrogen and phosphorus in water bodies?
    Answer: Eutrophication
  • Q8. Which health condition is commonly associated with air pollution?
    Answer: Respiratory diseases

MODULE 4

Repeated 2 Times

  • Q1. What is the colour code of the plastic bag used for disposing of microbial laboratory culture waste?
    Answer: Red

Important One-Time Questions

  • Q2. According to the Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016, what is a duty of waste generators?
    Answer: Segregation at source
  • Q3. Which type of waste includes leftover food, fruit peels and yard trimmings?
    Answer: Organic waste
  • Q4. What is the process of burning municipal solid waste in a properly designed furnace called?
    Answer: Incineration
  • Q5. The decomposition of biodegradable solid waste by earthworms is called:
    Answer: Vermicomposting
  • Q6. The liquid that passes through solid waste and extracts impurities is called:
    Answer: Leachate
  • Q7. Which type of waste is characterized as infectious and hazardous?
    Answer: Biomedical waste
  • Q8. What is the final step in the functional elements of solid waste management?
    Answer: Disposal
  • Q9. Which colour bin is designated for infectious biomedical waste in the Model Question Paper?
    Answer: Yellow

MODULE 5

Repeated 2 Times

  • Q1. What is Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) in e-waste management?
    Answer: Producer responsibility
  • Q2. What are the health hazards caused by e-waste?
    Answer: All of these
    Lung cancer, DNA damage, Brain

Important One-Time Questions

  • Q3. Which international agreement regulates the transboundary movement of hazardous waste, including e-waste?
    Answer: Basel Convention
  • Q4. Which toxic substance in e-waste is known to cause kidney damage?
    Answer: Cadmium
  • Q5. Which component of e-waste is valuable for recycling because of its precious-metal content?
    Answer: Circuit boards
  • Q6. Which hazardous pollutant is commonly found in e-waste?
    Answer: Mercury
  • Q7. What is the primary environmental problem caused by improper e-waste disposal?
    Answer: Soil and groundwater contamination
  • Q8. Which principle is important in e-waste management?
    Answer: Extended Producer Responsibility
  • Q9. The E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 introduced stricter:
    Answer: EPR targets
